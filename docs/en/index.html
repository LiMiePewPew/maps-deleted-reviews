What does Google count in this notice?

According to Google, these ranges cover reviews removed following complaints regarding defamation when the removal was processed within the last 365 days and was not later reversed. Reviews removed for other reasons are not fully represented by this notice.

Google's help page about the transparency notice ↗

What the numbers do not tell you

Google uses approximate ranges such as 21–50 or Over 250. If no notice was observed, that only means none was seen during this crawl. It does not prove that no review was removed during the last 365 days. Google Maps search results are also ranked and do not provide a guaranteed complete directory of all businesses. Individual review texts were not analysed for this project.

How was the data collected?

The crawler and source code are public on GitHub. The raw crawl is preserved. For the public page, obvious out-of-area results and profiles that are clearly unrelated to food and drink are conservatively filtered out. Ambiguous cases are not removed on a guess.

1. Search

Google Maps is searched using several food and drink related search terms for Osnabrück. The result list is ranked and is not a complete business directory.

2. Check profiles

For each discovered profile, the reviews section is opened and checked for the publicly visible transparency notice.

3. Merge and filter

Duplicate Google Maps profiles are merged. Obvious location and non-food results are counted separately and removed during the web export.

4. Store the observation

The range displayed by Google and the time of the check are stored. A discovery search term is not treated as a reliable business category.
